build Built

I have created the Café Coffee Day website prototype.

What Was Built

  • Editorial Hero & Brand Heritage (src/components/HeroSection.tsx, src/components/Navbar.tsx): A 3-zone top navigation bar paired with an asymmetric split hero showcasing Chikmagalur single-estate Arabica photography, active pickup café status, and a 4-column heritage metrics strip.

  • Interactive Menu & Customization (src/components/MenuSection.tsx, src/components/ProductDetailModal.tsx): Filterable catalog across Cold Classics, Hot Brews, Estate Beans, and Bakery & Bites with real-time search, an Iced Only toggle, quick-add actions, and a customization modal for drink size/pack weight, milk or grind preference, and add-ons.

  • Chikmagalur Craft Story & Café Locator (src/components/HeritageAndLocator.tsx): Brand origin overview paired with an interactive Indian café directory (Bengaluru, Mumbai, New Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad) supporting neighbourhood search, setting the active pickup café, and inline table reservations.

  • Slide-Over Order Bag & Checkout (src/components/OrderBagDrawer.tsx): Itemized order bag supporting café pickup vs. express delivery, quantity steppers, automatic GST calculation, guest checkout validation, and an order confirmation receipt.

Verification

  • compile_applet and lint_applet (tsc --noEmit) both passed with zero errors.
